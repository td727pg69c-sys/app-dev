# 🎬 My Favorite Series: True Beauty
## 📖 About the Drama
> *"True beauty is not about how you look, but about who you are inside."*
**True Beauty** is a beloved South Korean romantic comedy based on the hit webtoon. It tells the story of a girl who becomes a makeup expert to hide her insecurities about her appearance and the boy who sees her true self beneath it all. It’s heartwarming, funny, and full of important lessons about self-worth!
> | Character Name | Actor/Actress | Role Description |
|----------------|---------------|------------------|
| **Lim Ju-kyung** | Moon Ga-young | The cheerful student who transforms with makeup |
| **Lee Su-ho** | Cha Eun-woo | The quiet, handsome top student who sees the real her |
| **Han Seo-jun** | Hwang In-youp | The charming, tough second lead with a kind heart |
> ## 💡 Why It's My Favorite

- ✅ Beautiful story about **self-acceptance** and confidence
- ✅ Amazing chemistry between all the characters
- ✅ Both leads learn and grow throughout the story
- ✅ The soundtrack is **🎵 absolutely amazing**
- ✅ Friendship goals ~~and~~ plus family moments that hit hard
